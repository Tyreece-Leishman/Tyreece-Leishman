### 👨‍💻 My Coding Journey

My journey into tech started by accident. While messing around on a website one day, I clicked **F12** and opened the browser developer tools. I had no idea what I was looking at, but I was curious. After some searching, I discovered **freeCodeCamp**, where I began learning the basics of **HTML and CSS**.

That small moment sparked a genuine interest in technology and IT. I decided to take things further and studied **IT at college**, where I built a stronger foundation in computing and problem-solving. After college, I landed a role as a **1st Line Support Engineer**, which gave me real-world experience working with systems, users, and business tools.

In that role, I was exposed to **APIs, CRMs, and agentic AI**, and I started to realise that I wanted to be more involved on the **software development side** rather than just support. I enjoyed understanding how systems were built, how data moved between platforms, and how automation could solve real problems.

That curiosity pushed me to take the next step — applying for an apprenticeship. I’m now a **Software Developer Apprentice**, focused on learning, building, and continuously improving as a developer, with the goal of growing into a well-rounded software engineer.
